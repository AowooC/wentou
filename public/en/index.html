FAQ
What does Wentou do? It flags vague or confusable spots in your psychology notes, asks 2–3 probing questions per spot, judges whether you really understand from your answers, and outputs a list of open questions for your professor or review.
Who is it for? University students taking psychology courses anywhere in the world, from Psych 101 to developmental, social, cognitive or research methods. It only supports psychology notes.
Which languages are supported? The interface is in English and Chinese. Questions, feedback and the final list are written in the same language as your notes.
Is it free? Yes, currently 5 AI analyses per network per day. After that an offline demo is available (Chinese intro-psych only).
Are my notes stored? No. Notes and answers are sent to the AI service (OpenAI) only to process your request and are not stored on our server. Only feedback you choose to submit is saved.
Is the AI always right? No. Wentou is a study aid and can make mistakes; your textbook and instructor are the authority.


Disclaimer: Wentou is a study aid. AI-generated highlights, questions and judgments may be wrong and are not authoritative answers or grades; rely on your textbook and instructor. Sample notes are made-up data.
Privacy: Notes, images and answers you submit are sent to an AI service (OpenAI API) only to process that request and are not stored on our server; logs contain anonymized request statistics only. Feedback (“useful / not useful” and comments) you choose to submit is stored to improve the product. Please do not upload sensitive personal information.
